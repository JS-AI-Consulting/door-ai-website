Is this a fit?

We'll tell you if we're not right for you.

AI isn't the answer for everything. If it isn't for you yet, we'll say so at the Check-Up.

A good fit

  • 5 to 49 staff, or a growing firm up to 500
  • Too much time lost to emails, quotes, scheduling and paperwork
  • Willing to try a new way of working
  • Someone on your team who can own the change

Not a fit

  • You want a brand-new AI product built from scratch to sell
  • No one has time to own the change after we leave
  • You're looking for computer, network or phone support

Your data

Your information stays yours.

  • You own every account and tool. Vendors bill you directly.
  • We choose AI tools that don't train on your data, and we set them up that way.
  • We only access what a workflow needs, and you can remove our access any time.

Questions we hear

What does it cost?
The Check-Up is free. After that, every step has a fixed fee we agree on before we start, sized to your business, with no hourly billing. The Roadmap fee is credited toward your Setup.
How much of my time does it take?
About an hour for the Check-Up, a few hours of interviews for the Roadmap, and short check-ins during a Setup. We do the rest.
What if it doesn't work?
We test every workflow with your team before we call it done, and Care covers fixes after that. If a Roadmap shows AI won't pay off, we'll tell you plainly.
In person or remote?
Both. We come to you anywhere in Northeast Wisconsin, and we work by video with businesses anywhere in the US.
The UW and the Chamber offer free AI classes. How are you different?
Those are a great start. Classes teach the ideas; we do the setup inside your business and keep it running afterward.
Do we need to be tech-savvy?
No. We pick tools your team can actually use, then train them.
